How to delete your data

Version 1 · effective 21 November 2026 · Chrono Creator is a product of Chrono Consulting Group LLC, a New York LLC.

You can delete your Chrono Creator data at any time.

In the app: Settings → Privacy → Delete my data. We'll confirm by email.

By email: write to chronocreator@proton.me from your account email, with the subject "Delete my data".

What happens next:

  • We delete your account, your videos and their analysis, data from your connected accounts, and any data derived from your face or voice, within 30 days.
  • Some records we have to keep by law, such as payout and tax records. We keep a log that your request was carried out, but not the deleted data itself.
  • If you're a creator who shared results with a brand, we stop sharing straight away. Data the brand already received is under the brand's control.

Removing our access on each platform:

  • Instagram / Facebook: Settings → Apps and Websites → Chrono Creator → Remove.
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions → Chrono Creator → Remove access.
  • YouTube / Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions → Chrono Creator → Remove access.
  • Shopify (brands): Shopify admin → Settings → Apps → Chrono Creator → Uninstall. We delete the store's data within 30 days of uninstall, as Shopify requires.

When you remove access on a platform, we delete the data we got from it within 7 days.

Questions: chronocreator@proton.me
