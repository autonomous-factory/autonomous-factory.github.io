Brand (Merchant) Data Processing Addendum

Version 1 · effective 21 November 2026 · Chrono Creator is a product of Chrono Consulting Group LLC, a New York LLC.

This Addendum forms part of the Terms of Service between Chrono Consulting Group LLC ("we") and the brand ("you") using Chrono Creator, and covers data from your connected stores and accounts: Shopify, TikTok Shop, Meta and affiliate tools ("Brand Data").

1. Roles

You are the controller of Brand Data, including your customers' data. We process it only on your documented instructions: these Terms, this Addendum and your settings in the app.

2. What we process, and why

Data Purpose
Orders, line items, totals, discount codes, referral codes, UTM and landing data (customerJourneySummary) Attributing sales to creators and videos
Products and variants Showing which products each creator sells
Affiliate and creator records (codes, handles, commissions) Linking creators to their sales
Partnership-ad and branded-content performance Showing creator and video performance

We don't request customer names, emails, phone numbers or addresses (Shopify Level 2 data). If a future feature needs them, we'll ask you first and update this Addendum.

3. Our commitments

  1. Minimum data: we process only what's listed above.
  2. Purpose limitation: we use Brand Data only to provide Chrono Creator to you.
  3. No sale or sharing: we never sell, rent or share Brand Data, including anonymous, aggregate or derived forms.
  4. No transfers: no transfer to any third party or to any other app we own, except subprocessors who help us provide the service (section 5), or where you or the platform expressly authorise it, or where the law requires it.
  5. No AI training without your consent: we don't use Brand Data, including anonymous, aggregate or derived forms, to create, train, fine-tune or improve any machine-learning or AI model, unless you opt in through a separate, written consent you can withdraw. Running an AI model to produce your reports isn't training.
  6. No benchmarking: we never use your data to compare you with other brands, and never show it to other brands.
  7. Your creators: your business data is shown to your creators only if you choose to release it. Each creator sees only their own results.
  8. Customer choices: we honour the consent and opt-out signals passed to us (for example sale or sharing opt-outs). We don't make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on your customers.
  9. Security: encryption in transit and at rest, least-privilege access, access logs, and access checks before any AI model sees data.
  10. Retention: we keep Brand Data only while your connection is active. We delete store data within 30 days of uninstall or disconnection, and act on Shopify's customers/redact and shop/redact requests within 30 days.

4. Requests and incidents

  • We help you answer customer access, deletion and correction requests. Shopify's customers/data_request and customers/redact webhooks are handled automatically.
  • We notify you without undue delay (and in any case within 72 hours) after we become aware of a security incident affecting Brand Data.

5. Subprocessors

Our hosting, database and email providers, and, when used, a cloud AI provider (inference only, contractually barred from training on our data). We'll keep a current list on request at chronocreator@proton.me and notify you of changes in advance. You may object.

6. Audits

We'll answer reasonable written security questionnaires once a year, and cooperate with any data protection audit required by Shopify, TikTok or Meta.

7. On termination

We delete Brand Data within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep it, and confirm deletion on request.

8. Platform terms

Platform terms that protect Brand Data, such as the Shopify API Terms, TikTok Shop Partner terms and Meta Platform Terms, also bind us. Where this Addendum and those terms differ, the stricter rule applies. Nothing here requires exclusivity or restricts you from using other apps.
