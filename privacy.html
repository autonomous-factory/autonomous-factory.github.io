Privacy Policy

Version 1 · effective 21 November 2026 · Chrono Creator is a product of Chrono Consulting Group LLC, a New York LLC.

Effective date: 21 November 2026

Chrono Consulting Group LLC ("we", "us") makes Chrono Creator, an app that coaches creators to make videos that sell and shows brands which creators and videos drive sales. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and your choices.

1. Who this covers

  • Creators: affiliates, UGC makers and sellers who use the app. You must be 18 or older. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18, and we delete it if we find out.
  • Brand users: people who use the app on behalf of a brand.
  • Visitors to our website.

2. What we collect

From you directly

  • Account details: name, email, date of birth (to confirm you're 18+), password or sign-in method.
  • Videos you upload, and what you tell us about them.
  • Consent records: what you agreed to, when, and the version of the wording.
  • Messages you send us.

From accounts you connect (only with your permission, and only the permissions you approve)

  • TikTok: your basic profile (display name, avatar, open ID), profile stats (followers, likes) and your public videos and their stats (views, likes, comments, shares). For TikTok Shop creators: your affiliate orders and the performance of your shoppable videos.
  • Instagram / Meta: your professional account profile, your media and its insights (such as reach, views and watch time), and audience insights at the level Meta provides.
  • YouTube (when we add it): your channel and video statistics. We use YouTube API Services; see section 9.

From brands that connect their accounts

  • Shopify: orders, line items, discount codes and the referral details needed to attribute sales to creators. Customer personal details only where needed for attribution, minimised and protected as Shopify requires.
  • TikTok Shop: affiliate orders, including the creator and the content that drove each sale.
  • Meta: partnership-ad and branded-content performance.
  • Affiliate tools the brand uses (for example Social Snowball or Trybe): creator, code and payout records.

Automatically: device and log data (IP address, browser, pages, timestamps) for security and to keep the service running. We use only essential cookies (to keep you signed in). We don't use advertising trackers.

3. Face and voice data (biometric policy)

This section is our public written policy on biometric data.

  • What: if you opt in to delivery coaching, we analyse your eye contact, gaze direction, facial expression, energy, and vocal pace and tone in videos you give us.
  • What we never do: identify you from your face or voice, match faces or voices across people or videos, or create or store a face geometry template or voiceprint. We never sell, lease, trade or otherwise profit from biometric identifiers or biometric information.
  • Consent: we only do this after you sign a separate written (electronic) consent. You can say no and still use the app.
  • Retention and destruction: we destroy any data derived from your face or voice within 30 days of whichever comes first: you withdraw consent, you close your account, the purpose is met, or 1 year passes after your last interaction with us. In no case do we keep it more than 3 years after your last interaction.
  • Disclosure: only to service providers that process it for us under contract (section 5), to brands you choose to share your results with, or where the law requires it.
  • Protection: we store it with at least the care we use for our other confidential data, encrypted in transit and at rest.

4. How we use data

  • To provide coaching: video breakdowns, next-video suggestions, scores.
  • To show brands which creators and videos drive their sales (for creators, only with brands you share with).
  • To attribute sales to creators and videos.
  • To keep the service secure, fix problems, and meet legal obligations.

We don't:

  • sell or rent personal data, or data from any connected platform
  • use connected-platform data for advertising profiles
  • show one creator's numbers to another creator, or one brand's data to another brand
  • show a brand's business data to creators, except what that brand chooses to release
  • train AI models on Shopify merchant data, or benchmark merchants, without the merchant's consent

4a. Automated analysis

Breakdowns, scores and coaching tips are produced automatically: speech-to-text, on-screen text recognition, and coaching rules. They can be wrong. Treat them as suggestions, not guarantees. We label automated output in the app, and we're working to measure and improve its accuracy.

5. Who we share data with

  • Service providers that host, process or analyse data for us, under contracts that limit their use to our instructions: our hosting, database and email providers, and, when used, a cloud AI video-analysis provider that is contractually barred from training on our data.
  • The brands you work with, if you're a creator and turn on sharing for that brand.
  • The creators of a brand: only resources the brand explicitly releases (briefs, hooks, talking points).
  • Legal: when required by law, or to protect rights and safety.
  • A buyer or successor in a merger or acquisition, under this policy's protections.

6. How long we keep data

  • Copies of videos made for analysis: deleted within 30 days of analysis.
  • Data from connected platforms: kept only while the connection is active, and refreshed or deleted on each platform's schedule. TikTok Shop data is kept for at most 18 months, and deleted within 30 days of you revoking access (we aim for 7 days). When you disconnect or revoke access, we delete that platform's data within 7 days, or sooner if the platform requires it.
  • Account data: while your account is open, then deleted within 30 days of closure, except records we must keep by law (such as payout or tax records) and consent and deletion logs.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Access, correct, download or delete your data: in app settings, or by emailing chronocreator@proton.me.
  • Disconnect any connected account at any time, in the app or from the platform's own settings.
  • Withdraw consent to face and voice analysis at any time.
  • Depending on where you live (for example California, Colorado, Virginia, Illinois, Texas or the EU/UK), you may have more rights. We honour these requests for everyone, wherever they live.
  • We answer requests within 30 days, and don't treat you differently for using your rights.

8. Data deletion instructions

See also our data deletion page. To delete your data: open Settings → Delete my data, or email chronocreator@proton.me with the subject "Delete my data" from your account email. We confirm by email and finish within 30 days. To remove our access from Meta, go to your Facebook or Instagram settings → Apps and Websites → remove Chrono Creator. On TikTok, go to Settings → Security → Manage app permissions.

9. Platform-specific terms

  • YouTube (when we add it): we use YouTube API Services. By connecting YouTube you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's use of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke our access at Google security settings.
  • TikTok, Meta and Shopify: we use their data only for the purposes in this policy, and follow their developer terms.

10. Security

Encryption in transit and at rest, least-privilege access, access checks before any AI model sees data, and logging. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you, we'll notify you as the law requires.

11. Changes

If we make a material change, we'll tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect. Changes to the biometric section always need your new consent.
